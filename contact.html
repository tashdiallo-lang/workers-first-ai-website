Research

Research & policy inquiries

Researchers, policymakers, and advocates can ask about the report's methodology, findings, or policy framework, or request de-identified aggregate findings, methodology materials or a data dictionary, subject to privacy and consent safeguards.

Press

Media requests

Journalists can request interviews with the project team, the financial model behind the revenue estimates, or high-resolution photography. We aim to respond to press inquiries within one business day.

Drivers

Share your experience

Are you a rideshare or taxi driver? If robotaxis are expanding — or being debated — where you drive, we want to hear what's happening and connect you with driver organizing already underway.

Partnership

Partnership & support

Elected officials, unions, worker organizations, academics, and companies can back the next phase of research or add their name in support of the five policy components, in full or in part. Funders and partners retain no authority over findings or recommendations — every contribution is disclosed.
